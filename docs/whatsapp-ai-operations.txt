WHATSAPP AI — OPERATOR RUNBOOK (2026-11-07)

Status and scope
---------------
The application implementation is gated OFF by default. A passing mock protocol
suite is not a real Gupshup/Meta/FreedomPay pilot. Do not enable customer sending
or paid packs until the external prerequisites and the pilot below are complete.
No customer stock, financial documents, existing subscription contracts or
lifetime grants are rewritten by this module.

Commercial behavior
-------------------
BUSINESS / ENTERPRISE: 1,000 delivered outbound service text messages per
organization per calendar month, Asia/Bishkek. Stores, numbers and employees
share this balance. Existing explicit lifetime grants remain effective.
Unpaid self-service trials, expired subscriptions and STARTER cannot send.
Read history, stop, human takeover, archive and local erasure remain available.

Extra pack: 1,000 messages / 3,490 KGS. One-time FreedomPay payment; no recurring
card debit. Up to 10 packs per checkout. Included units are reserved first, then
paid lots by earliest expiry. Paid lots expire 12 months after verified payment,
not at monthly renewal. A delivery belongs to the send reservation's month.
A technical retry or repeated receipt does not debit another unit. Manual API
replies from Bazaar also count; messages typed directly in WhatsApp Business
are recorded as staff echoes and do not consume Bazaar reply credit.
Never change a sold offer entry in src/lib/whatsapp-ai.ts. Add a new version;
old pending invoices must retain their original amount and units.

Supported launch flow: KG recipients (+996), 24-hour service window, text and
normalized customer images. Other countries, voice notes, documents, video,
complaints, unsupported questions and missing authoritative information go to
an employee. No marketing templates, automatic transcription, calls or stock /
order / payment / refund writes by the language model. Product search uses the
selected store, current retail prices, discounts and public assortment only.
A public catalog link can lead to the existing checkout; no reservation or
payment success is invented inside the chat.

Before enabling
---------------
1. Get Gupshup ISV / partner approval for a Kyrgyz individual entrepreneur.
   Confirm in writing: Bazaar funds the provider/Meta bill; no customer Meta
   card; +996 service pricing; incoming AND outgoing BSP fees; fixed/minimum
   charges; top-up from Kyrgyzstan; overdraft policy; server-side inbound spend
   cap; support for embedded signup and the selected number's Coexistence.
   AI off does NOT stop the BSP's billable incoming traffic. An actual provider
   spend cap is a launch requirement, not a local Boolean substitute for it.
2. Confirm privacy / retention / cross-border processing and provider no-training
   terms for actual merchant/customer data. store:false is not a data-processing
   agreement. Obtain the merchant's appropriate onboarding permissions.
3. Use the currently documented Universal Token for partner operations, not a
   legacy 24-hour portal login JWT. Rotate it before expiry (maximum validity is
   60 days). The adapter uses Bearer for UT and the documented app token flow
   for app-scoped calls. Confirm GET app-token compatibility for this partner
   during the pilot. Never send any provider token to a browser or customer.
   An expired UT blocks new onboarding. Existing encrypted app-token transport
   remains usable; a DeepSeek outage pauses AI without blocking employee replies.
4. Run a persistent Node 22 worker near the primary database. Vercel request
   handlers only persist webhooks; they do not keep a background loop alive.
   Dockerfile.whatsapp-worker packages a non-root worker without .env files.
   Supply the SAME database, encryption key and provider configuration as the
   web app. Command: pnpm whatsapp:worker. Allow at least 90 seconds graceful
   shutdown. Database leases and opaque send IDs recover interrupted work.
   Worker heartbeat READY permits AI and manual replies; MANUAL permits only
   employee replies. Three independent conversations run concurrently, while
   every conversation's replies remain serialized. Turning AI off cancels queued
   AI answers, never an employee's prepared reply.
   In a container, invoke the installed runtime directly:
   node --import tsx scripts/whatsapp-worker.ts (the image's default command).
   The CI job whatsapp-worker-container builds the Linux image and verifies
   migrations, non-root startup, heartbeat health, two graceful stop/start cycles,
   absence of baked-in credentials and cleanup in an internal Docker network.
   Its disposable PostgreSQL has no host ports or customer data, and provider
   calls are disabled. Evidence is uploaded as whatsapp-worker-container.
   This proves packaging/lifecycle only; it does not establish live-provider
   compatibility, production capacity or the actual host's monthly cost.
   Before starting a permanent instance, confirm the intended hosting account,
   its resource price and database compute implications. The one-second queue
   polling loop can keep an otherwise sleeping database active overnight.
5. Obtain the FreedomPay test merchant. Approve/publicize pack terms, validity,
   refund handling and seller details before live collection. Existing legal
   publication checks also apply. Initial payment integration has no recurring
   automatic debit. Never enable a test merchant in Vercel Production.

Configuration (server secrets only)
----------------------------------
.env.example lists all names. Set WHATSAPP_ENCRYPTION_KEY to independent random
32 bytes as 64 hex digits. Do not reuse NEXTAUTH_SECRET. Keep a secure copy for
recovery; losing it requires reconnection. During rotation, up to two previous
keys may be supplied in WHATSAPP_PREVIOUS_ENCRYPTION_KEYS. Retire old keys only
after all connection secrets and unexpired encrypted media have been rotated.

GUPSHUP_UNIVERSAL_TOKEN and GUPSHUP_UNIVERSAL_TOKEN_EXPIRES_AT belong to Bazaar,
not individual merchants. App credentials and webhook authentication secrets
are encrypted with tenant/connection-bound AES-GCM. DeepSeek uses the existing
DEEPSEEK_API_KEY; OpenAI is not called by this module.

Each WHATSAPP_*_APPROVED / *_CONFIRMED flag represents completed real evidence.
Do not set them just to remove a warning. WHATSAPP_PILOT_APPROVED separates
manual onboarding/transport validation from AI automation. Pack collection has
an additional WHATSAPP_PACK_PAYMENTS_APPROVED gate. HARDENING_EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_MODE
=disabled blocks provider calls in isolated verification environments.
WHATSAPP_DAILY_BUDGET_USD_MICROS bounds estimated global AI expense; it does not
replace the contractual BSP/Meta cap or represent audited accounting.

Merchant UI
-----------
Operations -> Integrations -> WhatsApp AI.
Connection: choose store, open official provider onboarding, complete Meta's
business/number verification, then press Verify connection in Bazaar. No client
API keys are entered. A consumed/expired onboarding nonce cannot be reused.
Assistant: select one of six styles, language, address form and length.
Knowledge: publish reviewed address/hours/delivery/payment/return rules and FAQ.
Do not manually copy product prices/stock into free text. Preview uses genuine
DeepSeek once its data-processing gate is approved, sends no WhatsApp message,
uses no customer reply unit, and is limited to 20 tests/org/day.
Conversations: independent Bazaar unread state, explicit employee takeover,
resume AI, manual reply, archive/restore and confirmed local erasure.
Replies and billing: monthly balance, reserved deliveries, paid credit, pack
purchase, resume pending checkout and verified payment status.

Real-number pilot (required; not substituted by automated tests)
--------------------------------------------------------------
- One consenting synthetic/pilot merchant number; verify partner-owned billing.
- Actual +996 inbound text, price/stock/variant search and published FAQ in
  Russian, Kyrgyz and English; photograph matching; unknown product and employee
  handoff. Use real examples beyond a fixed two-command demonstration.
- Standard onboarding and Coexistence separately, if supported by the number.
  Check staff mobile echoes stop AI; merely reading does not send read/typing.
- Actual delivered, read, failed and delayed receipts; preserve opaque callback
  data. Simulate a connection timeout and worker restart; no automatic resend.
- Quota exhaustion, staff reply accounting, monthly boundary, purchased lots,
  provider wallet depletion and the provider-enforced incoming spend cap.
- A real sandbox FreedomPay payment, rejected payment, duplicate callback,
  delayed server callback and refund. Redirect alone must grant nothing.
- Verify the number's BSUID-country payload before enabling phone-less customers.
- Verify actual media download URL/auth behavior and the provider's download rate
  limits; do not claim the fixture tests establish those merchant capabilities.

Recovery / support
------------------
An UNKNOWN send stays reserved because it may have reached the customer. Do not
blindly retry it or release its unit. Valid delivery receipts reconcile it even
if the HTTP result was lost; unmatched receipts require provider investigation.

An ambiguous provider app creation is PROVISIONING_REVIEW. Find the existing
app by the deterministic name in the partner portal. Operator command:
  node --import tsx scripts/whatsapp-recover-connection.ts CONNECTION_ID APP_ID
It checks ownership/name at the provider before binding. It never creates a
second external app after an uncertain first result.

An uncertain invoice keeps the organization's pending slot. Use verified status
reconciliation first; never clear it or re-initiate collection based on a user's
redirect screenshot. Refund/revoke events quarantine the remaining credit lot
for review. Financial refunds and disputes require provider/support processing;
there is no autonomous refund decision by AI.

Images: encrypted normalized thumbnails, maximum 256 KiB, seven-day retention.
Text/customer identity: local 90-day retention. Explicit erasure removes local
name, phone, BSUID, external identity, text and attachments; financial delivery
counters remain. Provider/Meta copies require their separate deletion process.
Keep customer content and tokens out of logs, support screenshots and fixtures.

Emergency stop / rollback
------------------------
First disable AI and pack sales in web and worker environments, stop/drain the
worker and deactivate the affected provider connection if incoming fees must
stop. Changing AI mode alone cannot recall a submitted message.
Prefer promoting the preceding verified Vercel deployment while retaining the
additive database tables. Do NOT drop WhatsApp tables, credit or invoice records.
A source-code revert must retain this migration and its approved checksum in the
migration history planner; a blind revert that removes an applied migration
will correctly fail the deployment history guard. Reconcile outstanding sends
and payments before reactivation. No automated cleanup deletes customer stock,
subscription periods, invoice evidence or financial counters.
