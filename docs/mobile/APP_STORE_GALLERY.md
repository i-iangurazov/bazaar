# App Store gallery — fictional shop

The owner requested a professional public gallery with a realistic fictional
merchant, product names and coherent photography. Test-labelled QA captures are
excluded from that gallery. Customer, friend and existing payment-test tenants
are not used for the new shop.

The selected shop is **«Тёплый дом»**, with **«Центральный»** and **«Склад»**.
Its eight product photographs were generated with the built-in image tool,
inspected and saved under `public/retails/gallery-teply-dom-20261113/`.
The optimized 768×768 WebP files total 522,384 bytes. Original images, complete
prompts and per-file hashes are retained outside Git in
`professional-store-gallery-20261113/` in the local release evidence directory.

The isolated fixture uses normal receiving and cash-sale services, actual
server amounts and actual completion dates. It must not fabricate dashboard
totals or backdate transactions. Existing tenants and the migration ledger are
compared before and after fixture creation. Private credentials stay outside Git.

Gallery UI must come from actual app captures with device/build/hosted-source
provenance. Generated images are product data only. Editorial framing and short
headlines may surround an unaltered actual screen; they must not invent app
controls, values or features. Keep raw native captures separately from the
finished opaque gallery images. Target iPhone 1320×2868 and iPad 2064×2752.

Status: photographs and the guarded fixture plan are ready; the new merchant,
hosted-image verification, actual native capture set and final gallery acceptance
are tracked separately in the execution record. No gallery has been submitted.
